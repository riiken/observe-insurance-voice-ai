> Observe Insurance is a fictional company invented for this take-home
> exercise. The process and timescales below are illustrative and do not
> describe any real insurer's claims handling.

## Answer

Once a claim is submitted, it's assigned to an adjuster who reviews it and may ask for supporting documents. After the review, we contact you with the decision. Most straightforward claims are reviewed within two weeks, though it depends on the claim.

## Notes for maintainers

- "though it depends on the claim" is doing real work: it stops the two-week
  figure from being heard as a commitment. The agent must never guarantee a
  timescale or an outcome.
- This answer describes the process in general. A specific claim's status comes
  from `get_claim_status`, which requires a verified caller — never from here.
