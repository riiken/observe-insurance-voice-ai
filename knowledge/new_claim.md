> ⚠️ **DEMO CONTENT — NOT REAL COMPANY POLICY.**
> Observe Insurance is a fictional company invented for this take-home
> exercise. The process below is illustrative. It is not how any real insurer
> handles claims, and the website mentioned does not exist.

## Answer

You can start a new claim on our website at observeinsurance.com, or by calling our claims line during office hours. You'll need your policy number and the date of the incident. It usually takes about ten minutes.

## Notes for maintainers

- Deliberately does not promise that a claim will be accepted, or how long a
  decision will take. The agent must never make either promise (CLAUDE.md §16).
- This is one of the few things a caller can be told **without** verifying
  their identity: how to start a claim is public information.
